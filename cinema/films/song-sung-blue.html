Song Sung Blue

Song Sung Blue · Film

Published: 2026-01-10 · Spoiler note: This post contains spoilers.

Cinema Index Home

I just saw Song Sung Blue. I was surprised by how good this movie was. Mike and Claire Sardina are part of the band Thunder and Lightning. Mike is a Neil Diamond impersonator. The couple goes through many highs and lows: opening for Eddie Vedder, an accident that cripples Claire, Claire’s depression after the accident, heart disease, and an unexpected pregnancy involving Claire’s daughter.

Hugh Jackman is outstanding in this movie. His musical skills and dancing are outstanding. He portrays a charismatic Mike who struggles with different aspects of life.

I really enjoyed the use of Neil Diamond’s repertoire as emotional fuel in this movie. Yes, we all want to hear Sweet Caroline. My personal favorite Neil Diamond song, Silo, was missing. I enjoyed the mystic nature of Soolaimon.

Kate Hudson portrays a sweet, loving Claire. Her connection to music and her enthusiasm for sharing that passion through songs really hooked me. I couldn’t help but feel deep empathy for her struggles and her eventual return to the stage.

I felt the Eddie Vedder character lacked Eddie’s grunge voice from the nineties. In this movie, Eddie is portrayed as an amicable and almost sweet guy who meets Mike and asks him to open for their band, Pearl Jam.

The message of this movie is very touching. From overcoming alcoholism to the problems that arise in every couple, love and deep connection help you get through your woes together. I tip my hat to this movie’s production because it captures Neil Diamond’s song emotions very well: a bit of nostalgia, blues, deep hope for the future with optimism, tender love, happiness, and the enjoyment of being alive.
