Song Sung Blue

Song Sung Blue · Film

January 10, 2026 · Spoiler note: This post contains spoilers.

Home Film & Series

I just saw Song Sung Blue. I was surprised by how enjoyable this movie is. Mike and Claire Sardina are part of the band Thunder and Lightning. Mike is a Neil Diamond impersonator. The couple goes through many highs and lows: opening for Eddie Vedder, an accident that cripples Claire, Claire’s depression after the accident, heart disease, and an unexpected pregnancy involving Claire’s daughter.

Hugh Jackman is outstanding in this movie, both in his musical skills and his dancing. He portrays a charismatic Mike who struggles with different aspects of life.

What I liked most was how Neil Diamond’s songs guide the emotions of the film. Of course, Sweet Caroline is there, as expected. My personal favorite, Silo, was missing, but I did enjoy the mystic feel of Soolaimon. And then there is I’m a Believer. I have always thought it is a wonderful and memorable song. Used in Shrek as well, it captures that moment when you begin to believe again after a really bad love experience. It always makes me smile.

Kate Hudson portrays a sweet, loving Claire. Her connection to music and her enthusiasm for sharing that passion through songs really hooked me. I could not help but feel deep empathy for her struggles and her eventual return to the stage.

I felt the Eddie Vedder character lacked Eddie’s grunge voice from the nineties. In this movie, Eddie is portrayed as an amicable and almost sweet guy who meets Mike and asks him to open for their band, Pearl Jam.

The message of this movie resonates with warm emotions of unconditional love. From overcoming alcoholism to the problems that naturally arise in any couple, it reminds us that love and genuine connection are often what help you move forward together. I tip my hat to the film’s production for capturing the emotional spirit of Neil Diamond’s songs so well: nostalgia, a touch of blues, hope for the future, tenderness, happiness, and the simple joy of being alive.
