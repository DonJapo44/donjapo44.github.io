Project Hail Mary

Project Hail Mary · Film · 2026

Published: March 31, 2026 · Spoiler note: This post contains spoilers.

Home Film & Series

I connected with this movie’s emotional tone. I believe Daniel Pemberton’s score and other songs, such as the Beatles’ Two of Us, are very appropriate. We are used to movies with an apocalyptic view of destruction when meeting aliens. This movie flips that idea: what if love and friendship are universal and could be found elsewhere, far from our planet?

There are interesting details in this movie, such as how a thumbs-up sign could be represented as a thumbs-down sign in a different convention. It is powerful to realize how the other can create a visual representation of yourself. Dr. Grace recognizes himself in the little figure that is given to him.

I kept thinking about how different times are in 2026. I imagine that if this movie had been filmed thirty years ago, Rocky would be as culturally present as Wilson was in the movie Cast Away. As a physicist and scientist myself, it seems that there will come a time to expand human consciousness beyond our borders, maybe into the Solar System, maybe elsewhere.

I like Dr. Grace’s empathy.Having spent years working with high school students myself, I can recognize the empathy and kindness that Dr. Grace finds in them. This story is a reminder that an unlikely person can become a hero. Dr. Grace only lacked personal confidence, and he is able to recognize the best of humans in unlikely places.
