Context

This project began when I started studying physics through Landau’s Classical Mechanics. I was surprised by how differently the material is presented compared to the textbooks commonly used in Western universities. The exposition is concise, structural, and demanding from the very first pages.

These notes are simply an attempt to understand that approach more carefully and to record what I learn along the way. It is a long-term project. Physics is not my main focus at the moment, but I enjoy returning to it from time to time.
